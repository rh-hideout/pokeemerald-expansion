# Parallel player progression — core gameplay pillar

**Decision: LOCKED (2026-11-04).** Player growth occurs through several independent but interacting dimensions: Pokémon training, relationships and care, exploration knowledge, traversal capabilities, discoveries, and optional gym achievements. There is **no single mandatory global progression ladder** or badge/story requirement for enjoying the other paths.

**Geography interface:** [hybrid world geography](hybrid-world-geography.md) makes traversal capability and route knowledge meaningful without story/badge locks.

## Progression dimensions

| Path | Meaningful growth | Example evidence of progress |
|---|---|---|
| Pokémon training and battling | Improve a partner's capability and battle mastery | Levels, moves, evolving teams, tactical experience |
| Relationships and care | Build trust, familiarity and enduring bonds with individual Pokémon and people | Changed interactions, cooperation, remembered encounters |
| Exploration knowledge | Learn geography, habitat patterns, routes, risks and local information | Discovered areas, useful notes, known landmarks and safe routes |
| Traversal and preparation | Become capable of reaching more terrain through partners, experience and supplies | Reliable climbing, riding, swimming, expedition readiness |
| Discovery and collection | Find creatures, locations, mysteries and stories at a self-chosen pace | Pokémon records, observations, rare discoveries, lore |
| Optional gyms and trainer challenges | Pursue structured tests of combat skill and reputation | Regional badges, recognized victories, trainer relationships |

**Independent** does not mean isolated: a bonded Pokémon may help traversal, exploration may reveal new opponents or habitats, and a battle may change relationships. None must be a universal prerequisite for all the others.

## Locked principles

1. **Multiple valid careers in the sandbox:** a trainer, explorer, naturalist, caretaker or wanderer can make meaningful progress without switching to a compulsory main quest.
2. **No universal progress score:** do not compress all player achievement into a mandatory global level, badge total, reputation rank or checklist.
3. **Pokémon growth remains meaningful:** retain compatible levels, moves, evolution and battle mechanics; do not replace classic Pokémon development with an unrelated progression system.
4. **Relationships and knowledge count:** experiences beyond battle should have recognizable, persistent consequences rather than merely cosmetic counters.
5. **Capability is contextual:** traversal depends on terrain, Pokémon abilities, trust and preparation; discoverable geographic difficulty is allowed, compulsory story gating is not.
6. **Gyms stay optional:** eight-badge circuits apply to gym-bearing regions, but no global gym-completion requirement is imposed. The list of gym-bearing regions remains open.
7. **Avoid softlocks:** setbacks, missed events, scarce supplies or unavailable NPCs should not permanently prevent reasonable recovery or exploration.
8. **Progress is readable:** players should be able to understand relevant discoveries, relationships, abilities and optional accomplishments without requiring one giant completion meter.
9. **World autonomy remains intact:** the player is not the cause of every change; independent world events may create new opportunities or alter what can be discovered.
10. **Measured feasibility first:** exact UI, storage, records and unlock conditions remain uncommitted until the Expansion build is measured and a vertical slice is tested.

## Example sessions

- A trainer wins a voluntary battle and learns a new move, without earning a badge.
- A naturalist studies a skittish Pokémon's feeding habits, earns its trust and later recognizes it.
- An explorer charts a safe cliff route and returns better prepared with a climbing partner.
- A wanderer helps a traveling NPC and later hears about the consequence elsewhere.
- A gym challenger earns a regional badge while leaving the wider sandbox fully optional.

All are progress. They offer different rewards, not interchangeable rewards.

- [Hybrid wild encounters](hybrid-wild-encounters.md) preserves battle and noncombat interactions.

## High-level questions still open

- How exploration, bonds and discoveries are communicated through the player's map, notes and menus.
- How much capability should come from preparation versus explicit skill unlocks.
- How the initial playable slice demonstrates multiple progression paths.
- What existing Expansion mechanics and storage can be reused unchanged.

Do not resolve these through micro-system tuning until the player experience and engine constraints are validated.
